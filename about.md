I build things around a homelab called CMSNet — embedded firmware, custom PCBs,
milled-aluminum enclosures, and the software that ties them together. This site
is a lab notebook, not a publication: entries document real build progress,
including the dead ends.

Everything here is hobbyist work, unmonetized, and frequently built on the
shoulders of open-source projects that are credited in each post and repo.
Posts are AI-assisted where noted; judgment and errors are my own.

**House rules for what gets published:** no internal IPs, no network topology,
no device serials, no camera/automation details. Architecture yes, map no.
